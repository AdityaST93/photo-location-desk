      5

      Use the shadows

      Measure a vertical object and its shadow on flat ground in the photo (any unit, same for both). The tool finds the local times when the sun was that high and which way the shadow pointed.

      The dial shows the direction the shadow points, measured clockwise from true north. Compare it with a road or building of known direction on a map.
